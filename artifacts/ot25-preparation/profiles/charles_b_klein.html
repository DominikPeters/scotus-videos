About Charles

Chuck is a first-chair trial and appellate lawyer with more than 30 years of experience who litigates significant patent, antitrust, and commercial disputes. He has extensive experience representing clients in the pharmaceutical and biotechnology industries. This includes litigating billion-dollar patent and antitrust issues, such as successfully representing drug manufacturers as lead counsel in high-stakes patent proceedings related to mRNA technology, the Biologics Price Competition and Innovation Act (BPCIA), and the Hatch-Waxman Act. Eight of Chuck’s first-chair victories have been mentioned in the “Litigator of the Week” column published by The American Lawyer.

Chuck recently argued a case before the U.S. Supreme Court addressing the pleading standard for induced patent infringement, obtaining a unanimous reversal in his client’s favor. During his career, he has argued about 20 appeals, with many successful arguments before the Federal and D.C. Circuits in particular.  He has been actively involved in three merits appeals at the U.S. Supreme Court, all three of which resulted in 9-0 reversals.

Chuck, who was recognized as a finalist for Law.com’s prestigious 2026 D.C. Legal Awards’ Attorney of the Year, is the litigation office head in Washington, D.C. and was a longtime member of the firm’s Executive Committee, co-chaired the firm’s Intellectual Property Practice, and chaired the firm’s Commercial Litigation Practice. Chuck is ranked by Chambers USA for Intellectual Property and is a fellow of the Litigation Counsel of America. IAM, which ranks him in the Gold tier, described him as a “talisman for major pharmaceutical and biotechnology businesses” who is “no stranger to billion-dollar cases.” Managing Intellectual Property likewise recognized him as an “IP Star.”

Select examples of Chuck’s representative experience include:

  • Argued and won a 9-0 victory at the U.S. Supreme Court for Hikma Pharmaceuticals in a landmark case addressing the standard for induced patent infringement.

  • As lead trial and appellate counsel, obtained a trial judgment, affirmed on appeal, of noninfringement related to a stress-test agent.

  • As lead trial and appellate counsel, obtained a trial judgment, affirmed on appeal, invalidating six patents related to a drug used to treat very high triglyceride levels.

  • As lead trial and appellate counsel, obtained a trial judgment, affirmed on appeal, invalidating four patents related to a multiple sclerosis drug.

  • As lead trial and appellate counsel, convinced the Federal and D.C. Circuits to deny requests for injunctive relief related to a drug that treats gout.

  • As lead trial counsel, obtained a trial judgment of non-infringement and invalidity regarding an insomnia drug.

  • Obtained the first injunction under the Hatch-Waxman counterclaim provision—a significant ruling for the generic-drug industry that the Supreme Court ultimately upheld in a 9–0 decision.

  • Obtained a defense jury verdict in a US$1.7B pharmaceutical antitrust case identified by The Daily Journal as one of the “Top Verdicts of 2011.”
