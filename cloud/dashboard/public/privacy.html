SCOTUS uploader privacy policy

SCOTUS uploader is a private tool operated by Dominik Peters to prepare and publish Supreme Court oral argument recordings on accounts he owns or manages. It is not a service for connecting other people’s social media accounts.

Account information and permissions

When an account is connected, the tool uses the account identifiers, profile information, access tokens and permissions needed for the connected service. For Instagram, this means basic professional-account and media access and permission to publish photos and videos. The integration does not request access to direct messages or permission to manage comments.

The tool uses this information to identify the intended account, prepare uploads, publish content when instructed and check upload or publication status. It does not sell account information or use it for advertising.

Storage and service providers

The dashboard runs on the scotusstats.com web host; processing and media storage use Google Cloud. Connection credentials are kept in private configuration or Google Cloud Secret Manager, rather than in public source code or media files. Connected platforms receive the content and account information needed to carry out the requested operations.

The tool keeps media, metadata and operational records to support publishing, troubleshooting and reuse. Published videos, descriptions and artwork are public when the operator chooses to make them public; access tokens are not part of that published content.

Disconnecting an account and deleting data

You can revoke Instagram access in Instagram’s Apps and websites settings. Other connected services provide their own controls for revoking access. Revoking access does not remove posts already published on those services.

To request removal of stored connection credentials or account-related data, contact scotus@dominik-peters.de, identifying the account and the data concerned. The operator will remove the relevant stored data from this tool; content held by a connected platform can also be managed through that platform.
